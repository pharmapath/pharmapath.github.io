Pharmacy Knowledge • Scientific Research • Professional Updates

I am Ranjeet Kumar, a postgraduate researcher in Pharmacology with an academic background spanning D. Pharm, B. Pharm, and M. Pharm (Pharmacology). I completed my B. Pharm from Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU) in 2024 with First Division, and my D. Pharm under the Uttar Pradesh Board of Technical Education (UPBTE) in 2021 with First Division. I completed my M. Pharm in Pharmacology at Uttar Pradesh University of Medical Sciences (UPUMS), Saifai, Etawah, Uttar Pradesh, in 2026.

My principal research interests focus on experimental pharmacology, neuropharmacology, natural-product research, and preclinical models of neurodevelopmental disorders. My master's research project, conducted under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Mudit Kumar, Department of Pharmacology, Faculty of Pharmacy, UPUMS, investigated the “Pharmacological Investigation of Azadirachta indica (Neem) on Valproic Acid (VPA)-Induced Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) in Prenatal Wistar Rats.” This work reflects my interest in evaluating pharmacological interventions using established preclinical animal models and contributes to my broader scientific interest in neurological and neurodevelopmental research.

Ranjeet has developed skills in pharmacology research, literature review, scientific writing, pharmacovigilance, data analysis, documentation, and laboratory animal handling. His technical competencies include handling rats and mice, along with proficiency in Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint. His professional interests also extend toward pharmaceutical research and development, quality assurance, quality control, regulatory affairs, GMP-compliant processes, documentation, validation, and product development.

He has undertaken specialized training programs and certified courses relevant to pharmaceutical and biomedical research. These include a CSIR-certified project in Introduction to Pre-Clinical Regulatory Toxicology, an Animal Handling Training Program at UPUMS, a CSIR–Indian Institute of Toxicology Research (IITR), Lucknow Management Development Programme (MDP) on LEADS, and certified courses in Pharmacovigilance, Molecular Docking: Theory to Application, and Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP).

His scientific exposure includes participation in professional workshops and conferences. He attended a two-day offline workshop on 3D Bioprinting, organized by CSIR–Indian Institute of Toxicology Research, Lucknow, in August 2025. In January 2026, he participated as a poster presenter at the International Conference on “Artificial Intelligence and the Future of Pharma: Bridging Academia and Industry Imperatives” held at Constitution Club of India, New Delhi.

In addition to his academic and research activities, Ranjeet has a documented design registration with the Government of India for “Machine Learning Device for Detection of Biomarkers for Cancer”, registered under Design Registration No. 487025-001.

He has also gained practical healthcare exposure through six months of hospital training at Community Health Centre, Semariyawan, Sant Kabir Nagar, involving patient-care services, medication dispensing, first-aid management, wound dressing, routes of drug administration, and hospital record maintenance during his D. Pharm and B. Pharm training.
